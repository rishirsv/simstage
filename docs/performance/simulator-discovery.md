# Known-simulator connection discovery

Skip physical-device discovery when a simulator previously seen by this hub is
selected, while re-querying `simctl` to verify it still exists. Share in-flight
simulator and physical queries; retain fresh queries once the in-flight request
finishes. Create/delete invalidates the routing hint. Unknown IDs and physical
devices retain their discovery behavior; there is no timed device-state cache.

October 6, 2026, Bun 1.4.2, 30 alternating independent warm connection pairs on
the actual target host. Initial status establishes identity. Each sample includes
fresh simulator lookup, StartSession, and the first observation, followed by
disconnect. Clearing only the identity hint restores baseline parallel discovery.

Warm p75 improves 7.72 ms (1.2%); this does not support the plan's 100–500 ms
hypothesis on this unstalled host; the small timing difference is directional
rather than an established population gain. Ten independent warm `devicectl`
processes each consumed approximately 70 ms user+system CPU (10 ms timer
resolution, includes xcrun startup, excludes external daemons). Eliminating
those processes establishes the resource saving. The bounded change also removes unnecessary
physical queries and shares concurrent discovery. The stall case is verified by
controlled tests, not claimed as an observed field speedup.

Typecheck and 59 Apple checks pass, including concurrent discovery sharing,
fresh queries after completion, deleted-simulator revalidation, and routing while
physical discovery is stalled. Raw samples and commands remain under ignored
`artifacts/performance/implementation/discovery-results.json`, generated by
`discovery-benchmark.ts`; `discovery-cpu.json` retains the separate CPU samples.
